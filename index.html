I am a first-year PhD researcher in Computer Science, an ELLIS student at TU Munich (Campus Heilbronn) and the University of Cambridge, advised by Prof. Alexander Fraser and Prof. Anna Korhonen. My work is funded by the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML).

Originally from Belarus, I hold a BA in Fundamental and Applied Linguistics from HSE University and an MSc in NLP from Saarland University and the University of Lorraine, as part of the Erasmus Mundus LCT programme. I have worked as an AI / NLP engineer and researcher for +7 years in Russia, Italy and France — see career for the details.

Most of my work has been some form of AI for social impact, built where data and hardware are scarce, released openly, and judged by whether it can be trusted. Check out my interests, additional projects and publications pages if you want the specifics.
